# UX audit: mobile first

**Status:** all 23 items were built on October 28, 2026, except item 22 (Copy
note and CSV export), which is phase 2. See
`docs/superpowers/plans/mobile-audit-fixes-2026-10-28.md`.

Audited 2026-10-28 against commit `7cc0762`, before the Android and cloud sync
work in `docs/superpowers/specs/android-cloud-sync-design-2026-10-27.md`.

## How this was done

The app ran locally with seeded data (3 patients across 2 schools, 5 notes, 1
amended note, 1 deleted note, a provider profile, and a 12-day-old backup).
A headless Chromium drove every route at two sizes: a 360 by 780 phone
viewport (a Samsung Galaxy in portrait) and a 1280 by 800 laptop. Each screen
was screenshotted full-page, measured for horizontal overflow, and scanned
for tap targets under 40 px and text under 14 px. The wizard was driven
through all five steps, and the menus, modals, and edit mode were opened.

Rankings: **P0** breaks a task on the phone. **P1** makes the phone
noticeably worse than the laptop. **P2** is a design improvement that holds
on both.

## What already works well

- Every form field is full width on the phone, with 44 px inputs and
  readable labels. Nothing needs pinch zoom.
- The patient cards, the school list, the settings page, and the Recently
  Deleted tabs all fit the phone width with no overflow.
- The wizard's sticky Back and Continue bar stays reachable while typing.
- The objective categories are big tap targets, and the SOAP sections use a
  consistent color stripe on the wizard, the review, and the note page.
- Confirmations are in-app modals, feedback is toasts, and empty states
  explain what to do next.
- Amended notes show their badge and history; the export modal is clear.

## P0: broken on the phone

1. **Note page action bar overflows and Delete cannot be tapped.** On
   `/sessions/:id` the row "Back to Ava Martinez / Download Note / Edit /
   Delete Note" is a non-wrapping flex row. The page scrolls sideways to 450
   px, Delete sits half off screen, and the automated click on Delete failed
   because the Edit button covers it. The back link wraps to three lines.
   Fix: wrap the row (`flex-wrap`), shorten the back link to "Back," and on
   phone widths move Download, Edit, and Delete into the overflow menu the
   home cards already use, or a two-row layout.

2. **Note edit mode hides the end time.** In edit mode the date, start time,
   dash, and end time sit in one row that overflows to 502 px. The end time
   input is off screen, so a time correction on the phone needs a sideways
   scroll she will not know exists. Fix: stack date and times on phone
   widths, with the minutes row below.

3. **Patient page header overflows.** "Start from Last Session" and "Start
   New Session" are forced onto one line beside the name; the page overflows
   to 450 px and the primary button is cut in half. Fix: put the name on its
   own line and the two start buttons full width below it, stacked.

## P1: works, but worse on the phone

4. **The header eats a third of the first screen.** Title, tagline, four
   nav links, and an Add Patient button take about 220 px of a 780 px
   viewport on every page, and it is not sticky, so it is pure cost. Fix on
   phone: a one-line top bar (app name, or the page title) and a bottom tab
   bar with Home, Patients, Schools, Settings. Drop the tagline. Move Add
   Patient to the Patients page where it already exists and to a floating
   action button on Home if wanted. This single change does the most for the
   phone experience.

5. **The nav never shows where you are.** "Home" is hard-coded blue on every
   page, including Patients, Schools, and Settings. Use `NavLink` so the
   current page is the highlighted one. On the bottom tab bar this is the
   whole point of the bar.

6. **Tap targets under 44 px.** The four header links are 24 px tall. The
   patient card's three-dot menu is 28 px. The backup banner's dismiss is 18
   px. "View →" on the session history rows is 36 px. The app's own
   standard, stated in the README, is 44 px. Pad them.

7. **12 px text on the caseload.** "Last session: 10/19/2026," the school
   group counts, and the "Due" badge are 12 px. On a phone held at arm's
   length while standing in a hallway that is hard to read. Use 14 px
   (`text-sm`) as the floor for anything she needs to read, and keep 12 px
   only for badges.

8. **The wizard step indicator is clipped.** The sixth step circle is cut off
   at the right edge and the row scrolls sideways, which nothing signals.
   Fix: smaller circles with no labels on phone widths so all six fit, or a
   "Step 2 of 5: Objective" text line instead of circles.

9. **Long dates wrap into three lines.** "Tuesday, October 27, 2026" in the
   note header, the session history rows, and the Home date card all wrap on
   the phone. Use a short form on phone widths ("Tue, Oct 27, 2026") and
   keep the long form in exports.

10. **Toasts cover the header on the phone.** They are fixed top-right at
    desktop sizes. On a 360 px screen they land over the nav and the back
    link. Put them bottom-center on phone widths, above the sticky wizard
    bar.

11. **Recently Deleted title layout.** "Back to Home" and a two-line
    "Recently Deleted" heading sit side by side and look broken. Stack them,
    same as the Settings page does.

12. **Schools search shows two magnifying glasses.** The placeholder text
    starts with an emoji and there is also an icon. Remove the emoji.

## P2: design improvements, phone and laptop

13. **Cut the redundant wizard start screen.** Home already has the date and
    a Start Session button per patient. Tapping it lands on a second screen
    that shows the same patient, the same date, and another Start Session
    button. Go straight to Subjective. Keep the start screen only when the
    wizard is entered without a patient.

14. **Session times default to "now," which is wrong for after-the-fact
    notes.** The wizard sets start to 30 minutes ago and end to now. Her
    notes are written between sessions, so a note typed at lunch for a 9:30
    session saves 11:40 to 12:10 unless she notices on the Review step. Fix:
    on the Review step, default to the patient's last session times when
    copy-forward is used, and otherwise show the time fields near the top of
    the first step with a short "Session time" label, so they are set while
    the session is fresh.

15. **TherEx and TherAct minutes default to 0 and sit at the bottom of
    Review.** These drive the billing codes the exported note exists for
    (97110 and 97530), and a note can be saved with both at 0 without a
    word. Fix: prefill them from the session length split evenly, or from
    the last note, and warn on save when both are 0.

16. **The patient page buries what she comes for.** On the phone the first
    two screens are demographics (age, guardian, DOB, phone, diagnosis,
    grade, school, goals, notes) and the session history is below the fold.
    Her repeat visit to this page is to start a note or re-read the last
    one. Fix: name, diagnosis, and the two start buttons first; then session
    history with the last note's assessment line as a preview; then goals;
    then a collapsed "Details" section with the rest.

17. **The Home date card is a leftover.** It takes 250 px to show today's
    date twice (as text and as an input) and the Review step lets her change
    the date anyway. Fold it into a single line above the caseload
    ("Documenting for Wed, Oct 28" with a change link) or drop it.

18. **Add Patient form: required fields are split and a dead field wastes
    space.** First name, last name, and DOB are required at the top;
    guardian name and phone are required at the bottom, after seven optional
    fields. The "Age" box is a disabled input that says "Age will be
    calculated." Fix: put all five required fields first, show the computed
    age as plain text under DOB, make the submit button full width on phone
    widths, and consider a sticky save bar like the wizard's.

19. **"Due" needs a reason.** The badge appears with no explanation of what
    it means or how many visits are left this week. Tap or hover should say
    "1 of 2 visits this week." Also list "Due" patients first within a
    school group so the hallway glance answers "who's left."

20. **Patient page shows the legacy school name.** `PatientDetailPage` reads
    `patient.school`, a string copied at save time, rather than looking up
    `schoolId`. Rename a school and every patient page keeps the old name
    while Home and the Patients list show the new one. Read through
    `getSchoolById`.

21. **Age uses `new Date(dob)`.** The wizard header and the Review step
    compute age with `new Date(patient.dob)`, which `CLAUDE.md` invariant 2
    forbids because it parses as UTC and can be a day off. The patient page
    uses the proper helper. Use `parseAppDate` everywhere, and show
    "8 y 7 m" for the younger children where a whole year hides progress.

22. **Copy note and CSV export.** Already in the Android design; noted here
    so the note page's action layout (item 1) is designed with a Copy action
    in mind.

23. **Back links are inconsistent.** "Back to Patients," "Back to Ava
    Martinez," "Back to Home," and the bare arrow differ in length and
    wrapping. On phone widths use an arrow plus "Back" everywhere, and rely
    on the bottom tab bar for the rest.

## Suggested order

The P0 items are small CSS fixes and should go first, before or alongside the
cloud sync work. Item 4 (header and bottom tab bar) and item 13 (skip the
wizard start screen) change the shape of every page and are best done as
one pass with items 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, and 23, since they all touch the same
layout. Items 14 to 19 are each a small, separate change to one screen. Item
20 and 21 are bug fixes.

## What was not checked

- Real device testing. The screenshots come from desktop Chromium with a
  phone viewport and touch emulation, which gets layout right but not
  Samsung's keyboard, the Chrome address bar's effect on viewport height, or
  real tap accuracy. Both will show in the first week of use.
- Dark mode, landscape, and tablets.
- Screen reader behavior. Icon buttons do carry `aria-label`s.
- The PDF and DOCX output.

The screenshots from this audit are in the session scratchpad and were not
committed. Rerun the audit from the two scripts there, or ask for them, if
you want to see a specific screen.
